Chat vs. Dragonborn

Every command carved on this wall can be called from chat. Read on to learn how points are earned, and what each word unleashes.

How It Works

I.

Earn points just by being in chat — no grinding required.

II.

Spend them with !buy <action> to trigger something in-game, live.

III.

The bot confirms in chat once the game reports your command went through.
